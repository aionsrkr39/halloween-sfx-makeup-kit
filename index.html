  1. Shape the wound

  2. Blend the edges

    Use the included spatula tool to smooth and feather the edges so the wax blends into skin.

  3. Add texture with latex

    Apply liquid latex where needed for a torn or stretched-skin effect around the wound.

    Layer the fake blood in and around the wound with the stipple sponge for a realistic finish.
